Goals

Set your own daily, weekly and monthly targets. Hit your daily goal every day to build a Goal Streak.

How goals work

  • Pick what to track (tests, words or accurate tests) and a target for each period. Only typing you do after setting a goal counts toward it.
  • Your Goal Streak grows by one each day you complete your daily goal, and it appears on your Dashboard once you set a goal.
  • Weekly and monthly goals count how many weeks or months in a row you completed them.
  • Streak Freezes protect your Goal Streak too. With only one freeze, your normal daily streak gets it first.

How the rank system works

Your rank is based on your lifetime EXP total. Earn EXP from completed tests to progress through these ranks:

  • Whisper: 0–199 EXP
  • Breeze: 200–699 EXP
  • Zephyr: 700–1,499 EXP
  • Gale: 1,500–2,999 EXP
  • Vortex: 3,000–9,999 EXP
  • Hurricane: 10,000–19,999 EXP
  • Hound: 20,000–39,999 EXP
  • Catastrophe: 40,000–74,999 EXP
  • Atmosphere: 75,000–149,999 EXP
  • Jetstream: 150,000–299,999 EXP
  • Skyfall: 300,000–599,999 EXP
  • Zenith: 600,000+ EXP

Once you reach Zenith you can Seal Away. It resets your rank, EXP, Type Tokens and chests so you can climb again. Everything else stays, and a glowing pulse counter on your Dashboard shows how many times you have sealed away.

How the EXP system works

Every completed test awards EXP. The reward is calculated using your typing speed, accuracy, and test length, so longer tests and stronger performance can earn more EXP. Progress is stored locally in this browser and works offline.

How the streak system works

Complete at least one test each day to maintain your streak. The streak increases on consecutive days. Missing a full day resets it to Day 1 on your next completed test, and the streak EXP reward returns to its starting amount. The daily streak bonus is awarded only once per day.

Goal Streak works the same way, but it counts days on which you completed your daily goal from the Goals section. Streak Freezes protect it too, and if you only have one, your normal streak is saved first.
